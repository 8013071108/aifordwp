# Device Setup Stuck During Company Enrolment (L1 Self-Help)

Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

If your company setup does not complete, your files and account are safe.

## What you can do now
1. Keep your device connected to the internet.
2. Restart the device once.
3. Try the setup sign-in again.
4. If it fails again, stop retrying and contact the service desk.

## What to tell the service desk
- Your name and work sign-in name
- Device name (for example DESKTOP-FB099)
- Time the setup failed
- Any error code shown on screen
- Whether restart was already tried

Contact the service desk if setup still fails after one restart.
