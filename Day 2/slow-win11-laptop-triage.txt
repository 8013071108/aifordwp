Likely causes
- High resource usage (CPU/RAM/disk) from background processes (updates, indexing, sync clients).
- Endpoint protection scan or patching activity.
- Low disk space on system drive.
- Problematic startup applications.
- App-specific issue (e.g., Outlook profile/add-in) rather than full device slowness.
- Network-dependent slowdown (if only cloud apps are affected).

3 questions to ask
1. Scope: Is the whole laptop slow, or only specific apps (which ones)?
2. Timing/change: When did it start, and was there any recent update/restart/software change?
3. Impact pattern: Is it constant or only at login/startup/when on VPN-Wi-Fi?

First diagnostic step
- Ask the user to open Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) during slowness and report CPU, Memory, and Disk %, plus the top process by usage. This quickly confirms whether it is system-wide resource contention or app-specific.
