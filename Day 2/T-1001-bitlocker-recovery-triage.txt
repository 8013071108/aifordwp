TICKET TRIAGE — T-1001
Date: 2026-08-04
Analyst role: DWP Service Desk

======================================================================
SUMMARY
======================================================================
New Windows 11 laptop prompts for BitLocker recovery key on every boot,
preventing normal user access.

======================================================================
IMPACT
======================================================================
- Who affected : Single end user (new device, likely not yet fully
                 onboarded — business impact may escalate if this is
                 a day-one device for a new starter or returning staff).
- Urgency      : High — device is effectively unusable until resolved.
- Wider risk   : If caused by a policy or image issue, other recently
                 provisioned laptops from the same batch/build could
                 be affected (to-verify).

======================================================================
KNOWN FACTS
======================================================================
- Device OS    : Windows 11 (new laptop)
- Symptom      : BitLocker recovery key prompt appears on every boot
                 (not just once after a change event)
- Frequency    : Every boot — suggests the TPM measurement is failing
                 consistently, not a one-off trigger

======================================================================
MISSING INFORMATION TO GATHER
======================================================================
1. Has the user ever been able to log in normally, or has this happened
   from first boot?
2. What is the device make/model and serial number?
   (needed to check build batch — to-verify whether a known imaging
   issue exists for this model)
3. How was the device provisioned — Autopilot, manual imaging, or
   SCCM/Intune deployment? (to-verify)
4. Has any hardware change occurred (RAM swap, docking station, BIOS
   update applied during setup)? — any of these can cause TPM PCR
   measurement changes.
5. Is Secure Boot enabled and configured correctly in BIOS/UEFI?
   (to-verify on device)
6. Has a BIOS/UEFI firmware update been applied automatically during
   initial setup? (common trigger on new hardware)
7. Is the device joined to Azure AD / Intune, on-prem AD, or hybrid?
   (affects where the recovery key is escrowed)
8. Where is the BitLocker recovery key stored — Azure AD portal,
   on-prem AD, or MBAM? (to-verify with identity/endpoint team)
9. Are other laptops from the same build batch reporting the same
   issue? (to-verify with colleagues/ITSM)
10. Is there an Intune/SCCM BitLocker policy that could be enforcing
    re-encryption or conflicting with the TPM configuration?
    (to-verify with endpoint team)

======================================================================
LIKELY CATEGORY
======================================================================
Endpoint / Device Management > BitLocker / Encryption > Recovery Key Loop

Sub-category (to-verify): TPM PCR measurement mismatch caused by
firmware update or Secure Boot configuration applied during provisioning.

======================================================================
FIRST DIAGNOSTIC STEP
======================================================================
Retrieve the BitLocker recovery key from the appropriate escrow store
(Azure AD portal or on-prem AD/MBAM — to-verify which applies) and
allow the user to boot into Windows.

Once inside Windows, open an elevated PowerShell prompt and run:

    manage-bde -status C:

Review the output to confirm:
  - Protection Status (On/Off)
  - Lock Status
  - Key Protectors present (TPM, TPM+PIN, Recovery Password, etc.)

Also check the System Event Log and the Microsoft-Windows-BitLocker
event log channel for entries recorded at the time of the boot prompt
to identify the specific PCR or policy change that triggered recovery
mode.

Do NOT record or share the actual recovery key in this ticket beyond
what is needed to unblock the user. Follow DWP data-handling policy.
