TICKET TRIAGE — T-1005
Date: 2026-08-04
Analyst role: DWP Service Desk

======================================================================
SUMMARY
======================================================================
Teams audio is non-functional on three machines located in the same
meeting room, affecting calls and meetings from that room.

======================================================================
IMPACT
======================================================================
- Who affected : Three users/machines in a single meeting room — likely
                 impacts any staff using that room for remote meetings.
- Urgency      : High — meeting rooms are shared resources; a full room
                 being unable to use Teams audio blocks collaborative
                 working and may disrupt scheduled meetings.
- Wider risk   : If caused by a shared audio device (e.g. room speaker/
                 mic) or a room-level network/policy change, all
                 machines in the room will be affected simultaneously,
                 as reported.

======================================================================
KNOWN FACTS
======================================================================
- Application  : Microsoft Teams
- Symptom      : Audio completely non-functional (dead) in Teams
- Scope        : Three machines, all in the same meeting room
- Co-location  : All affected machines share the same physical space —
                 strongly suggests a shared peripheral, network
                 segment, or policy change as the root cause rather
                 than individual machine faults

======================================================================
MISSING INFORMATION TO GATHER
======================================================================
1. Is audio dead only in Teams, or also in other applications (e.g.
   Windows sound test, browser video)? (isolates Teams config vs OS/
   hardware)
2. Do all three machines share a common audio device — e.g. a USB
   conference speaker/mic (such as a room speakerphone or HDMI display
   with audio)? If so, has that device been checked/replaced?
3. When did the issue start? Was there any recent change — Teams
   update, Windows update, room AV equipment change, or Group Policy
   push? (to-verify with change management)
4. What does Teams show as the selected audio input/output device in
   Settings > Devices? Is it showing "No device found" or a specific
   device?
5. Has Teams been updated recently on these machines, and if so, to
   what version? (to-verify — Teams can auto-update)
6. Are the machines using the new Teams (Teams 2.x) or classic Teams?
7. Has a Teams admin policy been recently applied that could restrict
   audio device access? (to-verify with M365 admin team)
8. Do the machines have any pending Windows updates that could have
   changed audio driver state?
9. If a shared USB audio device is in use — does it appear in Device
   Manager on the affected machines, and without any warning flags?
10. Does audio work in Teams if a personal headset is plugged in
    directly to one of the affected machines?

======================================================================
LIKELY CATEGORY
======================================================================
Collaboration Tools > Microsoft Teams > Audio / Peripheral Issue

Sub-category (to-verify): Shared room audio device not recognised by
Teams (driver, firmware, or device selection issue); or Teams audio
device policy/settings reset by a recent update or policy push.

======================================================================
FIRST DIAGNOSTIC STEP
======================================================================
Test audio outside of Teams first on one of the affected machines:

1. Open Windows Settings > System > Sound and use the built-in
   "Test your speaker" function. Check that the correct output device
   is listed and produces sound.
2. If Windows audio works but Teams does not: open Teams > Settings >
   Devices and verify which audio input/output/speaker Teams has
   selected. Reset to the correct room device (or Default) and test.
3. If Windows audio is also silent: check Device Manager for the audio
   device — look for any warning indicators. Check whether the room's
   shared audio peripheral (if present) is detected at all.

This initial split test on one machine will determine whether the
fault is in Teams configuration or at the OS/hardware layer, and
guide whether all three machines need individual attention or the room
device is the single point of failure.
