TICKET TRIAGE — T-1004
Date: 2026-08-04
Analyst role: DWP Service Desk

======================================================================
SUMMARY
======================================================================
A company application fails to install from Company Portal with
error code 0x87D1041C.

======================================================================
IMPACT
======================================================================
- Who affected : Single user reported; if the app assignment targets a
                 group or device collection, multiple users may be
                 affected (to-verify).
- Urgency      : Medium — user is blocked from installing a required
                 application; urgency depends on whether the app is
                 business-critical for their role.
- Wider risk   : If the Intune Management Extension (IME) or device
                 enrolment is unhealthy, other app deployments on the
                 same device will also fail (to-verify).

======================================================================
KNOWN FACTS
======================================================================
- Platform     : Company Portal (Microsoft Intune managed deployment)
- Symptom      : Application fails to install
- Error code   : 0x87D1041C (reported by user or visible in Company
                 Portal)
- Device OS    : Unknown (to-verify — Windows assumed based on context)
- Error meaning: 0x87D1041C is associated with Intune app installation
                 failures; commonly linked to the Intune Management
                 Extension not being installed, not running, or the
                 device not meeting app requirement rules (to-verify
                 against Intune portal for this specific device/app)

======================================================================
MISSING INFORMATION TO GATHER
======================================================================
1. What is the name of the application the user is attempting to
   install?
2. What is the device make/model, serial number, and OS version
   (including build number)?
3. Is the device Azure AD joined, hybrid joined, or on-prem domain
   joined?
4. Is the Intune Management Extension (IME) installed and running on
   the device? (check Services or Programs and Features — to-verify)
5. When did the device last successfully sync with Intune? (visible in
   Settings > Accounts > Access work or school > Info)
6. Has the user successfully installed other apps from Company Portal
   on this device, or does every app fail?
7. Are there any requirement rules on the app assignment (OS version,
   disk space, specific registry state) that the device might not be
   meeting? (to-verify in Intune portal)
8. Has the device been recently enrolled, re-enrolled, or had its
   Intune enrolment repaired?
9. Is there sufficient disk space on the device?
10. Has the device been rebooted since the failed installation attempt?

======================================================================
LIKELY CATEGORY
======================================================================
Device Management / Intune > Application Deployment > Company Portal
Install Failure

Sub-category (to-verify): Intune Management Extension not installed or
unhealthy; device requirement rule not met; or app assignment not
correctly targeted to this device/user.

======================================================================
FIRST DIAGNOSTIC STEP
======================================================================
On the affected device, verify that the Intune Management Extension
is present and running:

1. Open Services (services.msc) and check for "Microsoft Intune
   Management Extension" — confirm it is Running and set to Automatic.
2. If not present, trigger an Intune sync via Settings > Accounts >
   Access work or school > [account] > Info > Sync, then wait
   5 minutes and recheck.
3. In parallel, check the Intune portal (endpoint.microsoft.com) for
   the device's app install status — the portal will show the specific
   sub-error detail behind 0x87D1041C for this device/app combination,
   which will indicate whether the failure is requirement-based, agent-
   based, or a download/install error.

Do not share device-specific Intune portal details (device names,
user UPNs) outside of the ITSM ticket.
