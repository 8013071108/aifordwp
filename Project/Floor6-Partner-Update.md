# Floor 6 Legal Incident Update

## Situation Summary
On Monday morning, a number of Floor 6 Legal users had trouble starting work. Some could not sign in at all, while others were delayed for much longer than normal. One user also reported that familiar desktop items appeared to be missing. Separately, one Legal user raised a concern about unexpected content shown in Copilot. Overall, this caused disruption to the start of the working day for Floor 6 Legal.

## What Happened
What we can confirm is that Floor 6 Legal had recently moved to a new desktop setup and that a new document-related application had been introduced before the Monday morning reports. We are reviewing whether that timing is relevant, but it has not been confirmed as the cause of the sign-in or desktop issues. The Copilot concern is being reviewed separately, and no conclusion has yet been reached.

## What We Have Done
We have taken immediate steps to reduce further impact while the affected group is reviewed. Support teams are working directly with affected users to help restore access, check whether sign-in is improving, and confirm whether desktop items return as expected. The separate Copilot concern is being handled as its own high-priority review.

## Current Status
This incident is not yet closed. The sign-in and desktop issue is still under review, and the Copilot concern is also still being reviewed. Users who are affected should restart their device once and try again. If problems continue, they should contact the Service Desk and share their device name, the time the issue happened, and a screenshot if possible.

## What Is Still Open
The exact cause of the sign-in and desktop issue is still being confirmed. We are also still checking whether the missing desktop items are part of the same issue or a separate problem. The Copilot matter has not yet been fully validated, and no conclusion has been reached.

## What To Expect Next
Updates will continue through the normal incident communication route. Affected users should contact the Service Desk if they are still unable to sign in, if sign-in remains unusually slow, or if important desktop items are still missing. Stakeholders should expect the next communication after the current checks are complete.
