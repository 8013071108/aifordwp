Title: Floor 6 Legal - Slow Login / Sign-In Issue
Version: 1.0
Date: 14/08/2026
Status: Draft

## Background
Some Floor 6 Legal users have reported sign-in problems, including very slow sign-in or not reaching the desktop. This started after recent device changes in the Floor 6 area.

The exact cause is still being checked. At this time, there is no confirmed evidence that your files or data are lost because of this sign-in issue.

## What You Can Try
1. Restart your device once.
2. Try signing in again and wait a few minutes before trying extra clicks.
3. If sign-in is slow, keep the device connected and allow it time to finish loading.
4. If you reach your desktop, check whether your normal work files are still available.
5. If sign-in still fails or stays very slow, stop and contact the Service Desk.

Please do not keep repeating sign-in attempts for a long time if nothing changes.

## When To Contact Service Desk
Contact Service Desk if:
- You cannot sign in after one restart and one retry.
- Sign-in remains unusually slow and you cannot start work.
- You can sign in, but the desktop does not load normally.
- The issue returns after it seemed to improve.

Support teams are actively reviewing this issue, but we cannot confirm a fix time yet.

## Information To Provide
Include the following in your ticket:
- Your full name and team (Floor 6 Legal).
- Your device name.
- The date and exact time the issue happened.
- What happened: sign-in failed, very slow sign-in, or both.
- The last step you reached (for example, password screen, loading screen, or desktop).
- A screenshot or photo of any message on screen (if possible).
- Whether one restart changed anything.
