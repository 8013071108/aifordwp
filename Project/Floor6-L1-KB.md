## 1. What is happening
Some Floor 6 users may have trouble signing in, may notice sign-in is taking longer than usual, or may see changes to desktop shortcuts. IT is working on this now and is taking steps to reduce further impact.

## 2. What you can do now
1. Restart your device once, then try signing in again.
2. If you can sign in but your desktop looks different, do not delete or recreate shortcuts yet unless you need them urgently for work.

## 3. When to contact Service Desk
Contact the Service Desk if you still cannot sign in, if sign-in is still unusually slow after restart, or if important desktop shortcuts are still missing.

## 4. Details to include in your ticket
Please include:
- Your name and username
- Your device name
- The time the issue happened
- Whether the problem is sign-in, slow sign-in, or missing shortcuts
- A screenshot, if possible

A separate Copilot access concern is being reviewed by IT as a high-priority access check. If you see unexpected content, do not forward or reshare it.
