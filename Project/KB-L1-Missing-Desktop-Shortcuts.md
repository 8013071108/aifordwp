Title: Missing Desktop Shortcuts
Version: 1.0
Date: 14/08/2026
Status: Draft

## What You May Notice
You may sign in and find that some desktop shortcuts are missing. This can make it harder to start work quickly.

If this happens, it does not automatically mean your files are gone. In many cases, the shortcut is missing but the file or app is still available.

## What You Can Check
Try these safe steps:
1. Restart your device once, then sign in again.
2. Wait a few minutes after sign-in to let the desktop fully load.
3. Open your normal work folders and check whether your files are still there.
4. Open the Start menu and search for the app or document shortcut name.
5. If you can find what you need, continue working and still report the missing shortcut so it can be fixed.

Please do not delete folders, move large numbers of files, or try repair steps you are unsure about.

## When To Contact Service Desk
Contact Service Desk if:
- A shortcut is still missing after restart.
- Multiple shortcuts are missing.
- You are unable to open a file or app you normally use.
- The issue keeps returning.

IT will review these reports carefully and treat them as important. We will investigate and guide you on the next safe step.

## Information To Include
Include the following in your ticket:
- Your full name and team (Floor 6 Legal).
- Your device name.
- Date and time you noticed the issue.
- Which shortcuts are missing (names if possible).
- Whether the files or apps behind those shortcuts still open.
- Whether restart changed anything.
- A screenshot of your desktop, if possible.

This information helps Service Desk check the issue faster and route it to the right support team.
