Title: Microsoft 365 Copilot Unexpected Content Guidance
Version: 1.0
Date: 14/08/2026
Status: Draft

## What Happened
A Legal user reported that Copilot showed client matter content they did not expect to see. At this stage, this is being treated as a serious concern and reviewed carefully by IT.

This report does not automatically mean there was a confirmed data breach. It means we need to check what was shown, where it came from, and whether anything needs to be corrected.

## What To Do
If Copilot shows content you did not expect:
1. Stop and do not continue asking Copilot about that content.
2. Do not share or forward screenshots widely.
3. Take one clear screenshot of what you saw, if possible.
4. Note the time and date.
5. Report it to the Service Desk straight away.

You can keep using your device for other work unless Service Desk advises otherwise.

## When To Contact Service Desk
Contact Service Desk immediately if:
- Copilot shows client matter details you did not expect.
- You are unsure whether what you saw is appropriate for your role.
- The same unexpected result appears more than once.

IT investigates these reports carefully and treats them as high priority, especially for Legal content. Access reviews may take place as part of that investigation.

## Information To Include
Please include:
- Your full name and team.
- Your device name.
- Date and time of what you saw.
- The prompt you typed (as close as you can remember).
- A short description of the unexpected content.
- Screenshot of the Copilot response (if available).
- Whether this happened once or multiple times.

This information helps IT review the issue quickly and safely.
