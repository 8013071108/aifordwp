# Microsoft 365 Copilot Prompting Starter Guide (FinBridge)
Date: 2026-08-13
Audience: Non-technical office staff

This guide gives ready-to-use prompts you can copy, edit, and reuse in your daily work.

## 1) A simple formula for better prompts

Use this easy pattern:

`Ask + Where from + How you want it + Any limits`

Example:
`Summarise this email thread + for a client handover + in 5 bullet points + actions and deadlines only.`

If Copilot gives a weak answer, improve it by adding one more detail (who it is for, time period, or output format).

## 2) Outlook prompt examples

1. Weekly inbox summary
- "Summarise my emails from this week into: urgent actions, waiting-for-reply items, and FYI only. Show deadlines next to each urgent action."

2. Draft a professional reply
- "Draft a polite reply to this client email. Confirm we received their request, ask for any missing details, and propose two meeting slots next week. Keep it under 120 words."

3. Prepare for a meeting from email thread
- "From this email thread, create a meeting prep note with: key decisions, open questions, risks, and who owns each next step."

## 3) Word prompt examples

1. Turn notes into a clean update
- "Rewrite these rough notes into a one-page status update for senior managers. Use plain language, short headings, and clear next steps."

2. Improve clarity of a draft
- "Edit this document to make it clearer for non-technical readers. Keep meaning the same, remove jargon, and shorten long sentences."

3. Create an executive summary
- "Create a two-paragraph executive summary from this document: paragraph 1 overall position, paragraph 2 key risks and next actions."

## 4) Excel prompt examples

1. Spot trends quickly
- "From this table, show the top 5 trends by month and explain each in one sentence in plain English."

2. Flag unusual numbers for review
- "Look through this table and point out numbers that are much higher or lower than usual. Put them in a short table with: what it is, value, and a simple note."

3. Build a manager-ready summary
- "Summarise this spreadsheet for a department manager: key numbers, biggest movement vs last month, and 3 actions to consider."

## 5) Quick tips that help every time

- Be specific about time period: "this week", "last quarter", "from Jan to Mar".
- Say who the output is for: "for my manager", "for a client update", "for the team".
- Ask for format: "3 bullets", "short table", "two paragraphs".
- Add a quality check: "If unsure, say what information is missing."

## 6) Data safety reminder (Day 1 habit)

Keep the Day 1 habit: use Copilot confidently in approved work tools, but do not paste confidential client or sensitive business data into external AI tools.

For day-to-day work in Microsoft 365 Copilot, you can continue as normal. If you are unsure whether something is sensitive, pause and check with your manager or service desk first.

A simple check before you paste:
- "Would this cause harm if sent to the wrong person?"
If yes, keep it in approved internal tools only and do not paste it into external AI tools.
