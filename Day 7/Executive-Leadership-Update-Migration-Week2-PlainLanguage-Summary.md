# Executive Leadership Update - Windows 11 Migration (Week 2)

The migration has largely landed well for most colleagues, and the overall experience is now significantly more stable than during the first week. Early disruption around sign-in reliability, missing files, and remote access has eased, and many teams are now reporting that day-to-day work is running smoothly. Staff feedback indicates that confidence is improving as key access and reliability issues have been addressed, and business areas are seeing better consistency compared with the initial rollout period.

A small number of issues remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printing problem is still unresolved and is continuing to affect local workflows, so this has been escalated for priority action. A newer concern has also emerged for Finance users, where large spreadsheet work is causing repeated interruptions; this is being treated as a high-priority business productivity risk. In parallel, targeted checks are underway on recent endpoint policy changes linked to slower startup experience in one business group, with controlled validation steps in progress to restore normal performance without introducing wider disruption. We will continue regular updates while these final issues are worked through.
