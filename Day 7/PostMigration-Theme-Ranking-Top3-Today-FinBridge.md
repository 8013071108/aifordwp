## Top 3 Themes to Act on Today (Ranked by Impact + Volume)

1. Missing files and OneDrive concerns (Count: 5, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks #1: Potential data-loss perception and inability to retrieve working documents creates immediate business continuity risk; blocker impact plus meaningful volume outranks all other issues today.
- Manager sentence: "We need immediate OneDrive recovery triage because users reporting missing files are blocked from critical work and this is our highest operational risk."

2. Sign-in and account lockouts (Count: 7, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks #2: Highest-volume blocker theme directly prevents access to AVD and user accounts; it is only behind missing files because lockouts are usually recoverable faster than potential file-access/data concerns.
- Manager sentence: "Account lockouts and AVD sign-in failures are our most widespread access blocker and require same-day remediation with identity and service desk escalation."

3. Shared drive access failures (Count: 3, Severity: Blocker)
- Why this ranks #3: Lower volume than some friction themes, but impact is full work stoppage on finance and reporting tasks, so blocker severity must outrank higher-count friction or minor items.
- Manager sentence: "Shared drive access failures are a smaller-volume but high-impact blocker, so we should prioritize permission and mapping fixes today to unblock finance workflows."

## Why Not Count-Only Ranking

A count-only approach would over-prioritize friction themes like printer mapping (6) above blocker themes like shared drive failures (3). For today’s action list, blocker severity is weighted first, then volume, so business-stopping issues are handled before high-volume inconvenience issues.
