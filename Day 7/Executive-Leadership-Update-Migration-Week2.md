The migration has generally landed well for most colleagues, and overall day-to-day experience is better than it was in the first week. Early access issues that were preventing people from signing in have now been stabilized, and many users are reporting smoother performance and a more reliable working day. Concerns about missing cloud files and unstable remote access have also largely settled, and feedback now shows a much more consistent experience across teams.

A small number of important issues remain open and are being actively managed. The floor 3 printing problem is still disrupting teams and is now being treated as a priority operational issue with escalation in progress. We are also seeing a new pattern where large spreadsheet work is unstable for some finance users, which is affecting productivity; this has been isolated as a focused workstream with targeted investigation and controlled fixes. In parallel, we are reviewing recent configuration changes for finance devices to reduce morning slowdowns without weakening security. We will continue to issue regular updates, keep business impact contained, and only close these items once user experience is consistently stable.
