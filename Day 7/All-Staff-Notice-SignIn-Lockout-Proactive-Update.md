Subject: All-Staff Update - Sign-In and Account Lockout Issue

We are aware of increased reports this morning of sign-in and account lockout issues (including AVD access). Our engineering teams are actively investigating and working to restore normal service.

While we work on this, please try this interim workaround:
1. Close all Microsoft apps, wait 2 minutes, then try signing in again using your full work email and password.
2. If sign-in works, restart your laptop once and then try AVD again.

If you are still locked out, please contact the service desk and share: your username, the exact error message, the time it happened, and whether this is your first or repeated lockout this week.

Please stay calm - we know this is disruptive, and teams are prioritising it now. We will provide the next update by 12:30 today, even if we do not yet have full resolution.

Thank you for your patience and cooperation.
