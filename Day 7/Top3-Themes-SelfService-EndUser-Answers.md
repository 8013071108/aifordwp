1) If you cannot sign in or your account says locked

Try these steps:
1. Close the sign-in screen and wait 2 minutes, then try once more with your latest password.
2. If it still fails, restart your device and try again once.

If you still cannot sign in, contact the service desk right away. Share: your username, exact error message, time of the last failed attempt, and whether you were on office network or VPN.

2) If your floor printer is not showing or not working

Try these steps:
1. Sign out of Windows, then sign back in and check the printer list again.
2. If still missing, print one test page to a nearby backup printer (for example another floor) so your work is not blocked.

If your usual printer is still unavailable, contact the service desk. Share: printer name/location (for example Floor 3), your device name, time of issue, and whether any printer works at all.

3) If your OneDrive files look missing

Try these steps:
1. Open OneDrive and check if it says signed in; if not, sign in with your work account.
2. In File Explorer, check OneDrive folders and search by file name before assuming the file is gone.

If files are still missing, contact the service desk immediately. Share: file/folder names, last time you saw them, screenshot of any OneDrive error, and whether this affects all files or only specific ones.
