1) If your files seem missing
Open OneDrive from the taskbar cloud icon and check that you are signed in with your work account. Then open your OneDrive folder in File Explorer and use search for the file name.

If still missing, contact the service desk straight away and share: your username, one missing file name, the folder you expected it in, and the time you first noticed the issue.

2) If you cannot sign in or your account is locked
Close all Microsoft apps, wait 2 minutes, then try signing in again with your full work email and password. If you can, restart your laptop once and try AVD again.

If you are still locked out or cannot sign in, contact the service desk and share: your username, exact error message, the time it happened, and whether this is your first or repeated lockout this week.

3) If you cannot access the shared drive
In File Explorer, open This PC and check whether the S drive is listed. If it is missing, sign out of Windows, sign back in, and try again.

If access still fails, contact the service desk and share: your username, drive letter (S:), the exact message on screen, and the business task you are blocked on (for example month-end report).
