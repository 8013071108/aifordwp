# Finance Shared Drive Not Showing - Quick Help

Version: v 1.0
Date: 07/08/2026
Status: Draft

Your files are safe. If your Finance shared drive is missing, this usually means the drive link did not load correctly after sign-in.

## What you can do now
1. Sign out of Windows.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Sign back in.
4. Open File Explorer and check if drive S: is visible.
5. If S: is missing, restart your computer once.
6. After restart, sign in and check drive S: again.

## If the drive is still missing
Contact the service desk.

## Details to include in your ticket
- Your name and username
- Your device name (for example DESKTOP-FB041)
- Time you first noticed the issue
- Whether restarting helped
- Whether drive S: is missing or shows an error when opened
- A screenshot of what you see in File Explorer (if possible)

Contact the service desk if drive S: is still missing after the steps above.
