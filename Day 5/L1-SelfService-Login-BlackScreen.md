# Blank Screen After You Sign In

Version: v 1.0  
Date: 07/08/2026  
Status: Draft

If your screen stays blank after you sign in, your files are safe and nothing is lost.

## What You Can Do Now
1. Wait up to 2 minutes after you sign in.
2. If the screen is still blank, sign out, then sign in again one time.
3. If it still does not load, restart your computer and sign in again.
4. Do not keep trying many times in a row.

## What To Expect
- In some cases, the screen appears after a short wait.
- If it does not recover, the IT support team can get you back in quickly.

## Information To Include In Your Support Request
Please share these details when you contact support:
- Your full name and work sign-in name
- The time the issue started
- Whether the screen cleared after a short wait or stayed blank
- Any message you saw (exact words, if possible)
- A screenshot (if you can take one)
- Your computer name (from the sticker or device info)
- Whether you are in the office or at home

## When To Contact IT Support
Contact the IT support team if the screen is still blank after you try the steps above, or if the issue keeps coming back.
